# Reel script

Read `references/content-bible.md` and `references/example-asd-ste100.md` first.

## Rules
- 130 to 170 spoken words. At 150 to 160 wpm raw and 1.15x speed, that is 45 to 60 s.
- One idea. One number the viewer can repeat.
- **Hook, first 3 s, word for word.** A personal claim, a surprise, or a cost. Not "Hey guys" and not a question.
- Proof by second 6: a named source, post, repo, or number.
- One honest caveat ("But there's a catch"). Always included.
- If the user tested it, add a "my test" beat with what actually happened.
- CTA: a real question, or "send this to someone who...". Never "comment X for the link".
- Short sentences. Say numbers the way they will appear on screen ("2,400 characters").
- Cut 20% after the first draft.

## Facts
Every claim gets a source next to it in the draft: a link, a timestamp, or "user's own test".
Check names, dates, versions, and numbers against the source before recording.
If a claim cannot be traced, cut it or mark it and ask.

## Output
Write `script.md` in the output folder with:
1. The script as a beat table: beat, line, source, picture idea.
2. A teleprompter block: one sentence per line, no stage directions.
3. Recording notes: about 32 pt/s scroll speed for a 60 s read, eye-level camera, phone back camera, one clean take with a 1 s pause before the hook.
4. Word count and estimated length at 1.15x.

## After recording
The take is the truth. Transcribe it (reel-edit skill), compare it to the script, and use the spoken words for captions and beat times. Flag any spoken fact that changed.
