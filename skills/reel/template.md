# Default reel template

Use these defaults unless the intake answers change them.

## Shape
- Length: 45 to 60 s after speed-up. Script: 130 to 170 spoken words.
- Speed: 1.15x, with pauses trimmed to 0.22 s.
- 8 beats, in this order:
  1. **Hook** (face, about 3 s). The claim, word for word. On-screen text repeats it.
  2. **Origin** (b-roll). Where it comes from, with a credit line.
  3. **Proof** (doc card or screenshot). The source with highlights.
  4. **How to use it** (prompt card or screen).
  5. **Result** (side-by-side, scroll card, or bars). One number.
  6. **The catch** (face, punch-in, stamp). The honest limit. No sound.
  7. **My test** (chat card), only if the user tested it. Otherwise a second proof.
  8. **Fix and CTA** (quote card, then face). A real question or "send this to someone who...". No sound.
- Face on screen 3 times: hook, catch, CTA.

## Picture by footage type
| Footage | Treatment |
|---|---|
| Face take | Graded, 9:16 crop, slow push, 2 or 3 word lines at the top |
| Video b-roll | `footage` fill, or card if wide. Title at the top, credit line at the bottom |
| Screenshot or post | `doc_card` with highlight sweeps and one callout |
| Two outputs to compare | `prompt_pair`, then `scroll_card` for each, then `bars` |
| A rule with two sides | `verdict` |
| Your own test | `chat_test` with a stamp on the key word |
| A quote | `quote_fix` |

## Look
- Font: Avenir Next. Heavy for captions, bold for titles.
- Colors: yellow #FFD400 accent on near-black #0E1014 and card #101217. Red and green only for bad and good.
- Captions: burned in, 2 to 4 words, above the Instagram UI safe zone.
- Motion: Material 3 and Carbon curves, springs on pop-ins, 4-frame staggers, directional blur on slides.

## Sound
- About 5 cues per 45 s, each from a different family. No repeats.
- Silence on the catch, the question beat, and the CTA.
- One off-screen ambience as a J-cut into the first b-roll.
- Every cue gets the shared room reverb and a 1 to 4 kHz dip, sitting 6 to 12 dB under the voice.
- Voice at -14 LUFS, true peak at or below -1 dBTP.

## Approvals
1. Script and beat plan
2. 540p preview (picture and voice only)
3. Full render with sound and passing checks
